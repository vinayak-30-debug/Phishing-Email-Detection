Hackathon Spotlight: Push to Prod - Building at the Frontier

Push to Prod is an in-person hackathon happening in Bangalore this August, in collaboration with Anthropic, Elevation Capital, and Mesa School of Business.

Builders are tasked with working on bold solutions for the Next Audacious Products from India: ideas that could turn into foundational infrastructures with the potential to scale globally.

This one-day hackathon is part of Basecamp, Bengaluru's week-long, community-led tech festival taking place from August 6 to 12, with a range of events happening throughout the week.
What to expect:

5 hours of in-person building with credits from Anthropic
Getting mentored by the ecosystem partners, building your best project, and competing for exciting prizes
Event details:

Dates: 8th August 2026
Happening: In-person, Bengaluru
Rewards: $10,000 worth of credits
Deadline to apply → July 26, 2026
